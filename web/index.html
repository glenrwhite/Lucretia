The LUCRETIA Project

A Matlab-based toolkit for the modelling and simulation of single-pass electron transport lines.

Lucretia is oriented towards the simulation issues that matter for linacs, bunch compressors, linac-driven FELs, and linear collider Low Emittance Transport — including short-range and long-range wakefields, bunch-train dynamics, and realistic representations of klystron, power supply, and girder relationships. See the Overview for the full picture, or jump straight into the Examples.
